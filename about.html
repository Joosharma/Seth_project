About Our School

A senior secondary CBSE school nurturing students from Nursery to Class XII.

Our Story

Building Futures Through Education

Seth Chhoteylal Academy was founded with a simple belief — every child deserves the opportunity to discover their potential. Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school in Rath, Hamirpur, serving students from Nursery through Class XII.

Our academic vision is grounded in the conviction that great schools build great citizens. We focus on strong fundamentals, character development, creativity and a lifelong love of learning.

Our Vision

  • Be a school known for academic excellence and strong character.
  • Nurture confident, compassionate, lifelong learners.
  • Stay rooted in our community while opening doors to the wider world.
  • Lead with discipline, creativity and innovation.

Our Mission

  • Cultivate discipline and strong values in every student.
  • Deliver quality CBSE education with caring and qualified teachers.
  • Build character through service, sports and the arts.
  • Encourage innovation, curiosity and independent thinking.
  • Contribute meaningfully to our community.
